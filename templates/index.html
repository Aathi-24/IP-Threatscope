Core Capabilities

Everything your security team needs

From single-IP lookups to scheduled bulk scans with automated email reports — IP Threatscope covers the full threat-intelligence workflow.

Multi-Vendor Intelligence

Query 70+ security databases simultaneously and get a unified view of every vendor's verdict for any IP address — in a single click.

Automated Email Reports

Configure your check interval and receive detailed blocked-IP reports delivered directly to any mailbox — fully automatic, on your schedule.

Batch IP Scanning

Manage and scan entire IP lists. Navigate per-IP results individually with full vendor-by-vendor breakdown and threat classification.

Deep IP Intelligence

Beyond reputation scores — get WHOIS data, ASN information, ISP details, network classification, and full abuse history per IP.

Threat Level Classification

Every scan produces a risk rating — Clean, Low, Medium, High, or Critical — based on the consensus across all queried vendors.

CSV Export & Auditing

Export full vendor results or filtered blocked-only reports as CSV files for compliance documentation, auditing, and SIEM integration.

Enterprise Ready

Built for professional security teams

IP Threatscope is designed from the ground up for enterprise environments — secure, flexible, and SMTP-agnostic.

Isolated Per-Client Data

Each account operates in complete isolation. Your IP lists, settings, and reports are never shared with other clients.

SMTP-Agnostic Email Delivery

Use any mail server — Microsoft 365, Exchange, Google Workspace, Postfix, or any custom SMTP — with SSL, TLS, or STARTTLS.

Hashed Password Storage

All account passwords are hashed using industry-standard PBKDF2 before storage. Plain-text passwords are never stored or transmitted.

Configurable Scheduling

Set your own check interval — from minutes to hours. The built-in scheduler runs your IP list automatically and emails the results.

Actionable Intelligence

Risk-level badges (Clean / Low / Medium / High / Critical), detection progress bars, and per-vendor drill-down make results immediately actionable.

Responsive on Any Device

Full-featured across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Secure sign-in from the field, full analysis from the office.
